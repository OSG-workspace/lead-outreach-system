# Brief

GCC AI-receptionist campaign. Target: BIG businesses with HIGH inbound call volume across UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Bahrain, Kuwait. Phone-driven verticals where missed calls = lost revenue: private hospitals, multi-branch clinics, 5-star hotels and resorts, luxury real estate brokerages, multi-brand auto dealerships, top-tier law firms, insurance brokers, pharmacy chains.

Uses the fixed user template (zero-cost / commission-only AI receptionist pitch). No template variation by signal. No em-dashes anywhere in body copy.

- Sourcing: `source-agent-worldwide` filtered to GCC ISO codes via `countries.txt`.
- Channels: email only.
- Send cap: governed by `MAX_EMAILS_PER_RUN` env var (default 1000).
