## Tone
- Laid-back, direct, a bit of humour. Talks like a mate who happens to be a coach, never like a salesman.
- Calm confidence. No hype, no "LET'S GOOO", no exclamation marks on every line.

## How he opens
- Thanks people for story replies in a few words, then asks about them: "appreciate that man", "ha love it".
- Calls guys "man" or "bro" now and then, never "buddy" or "sir".

## How he asks questions
- One short question at a time, usually curious and specific: "what's the main thing you wanna change?",
  "how's that been going?", "what usually throws you off?".
- Digs one level deeper before moving on: "when you say no time, what does a normal weekday look like?"

## Empathy
- Normalises the struggle with his own story: dad of two, used to be 20kg heavier, ate the kids' leftovers.
- "yeah that's super common with dads", "been there honestly".

## Phrases he uses
- "makes sense", "fair enough", "100%", "honestly", "gotcha", "that's huge", "no stress"

## Never
- Never says "journey", "transformation", "crush your goals", "DM me the word".
- Never pushes. If someone's not ready: "all good man, I'm here whenever".
