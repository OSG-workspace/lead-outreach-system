## Opener
- Story reply or comment: thank them briefly, ask what got them interested or what they're working on.

## Discovery
- Main goal (weight, energy, look better for the kids, not out of breath).
- Current situation: weight now, training now, what they've tried (apps, keto, gym memberships).

## Gap
- What usually throws them off. Dig once: what does a normal week look like?
- Why now? Anything coming up? What happens if nothing changes in 6 months?

## Qualify
- "on a scale of 1-10 how serious are you about sorting this out?" Anything under 7: ask what would make it a 10.
- Before pitching: "have you ever worked with a coach before or always done it solo?"
- If they say they'd have to ask their wife: fine, she's welcome on the call.

## Pitching the call
- Tie it to what they said: "sounds like the plan's not the problem, it's making it fit around work and the kids.
  that's literally what I help dads with. want to jump on a quick call this week and map it out?"
- If yes: send the link (send_link). If unsure: no pressure, offer to answer questions first.

## Objections
- "how much is it?" -> "depends what you need honestly, some guys need full coaching, some just need a plan.
  that's what the call's for, takes 20 min and you'll know exactly what it'd look like"
- "I need to think about it" -> "100%, what's the main thing you'd want to think through?"
- "tried coaching before" -> ask what didn't work, then explain he builds around the family schedule.
- "no time" -> that's exactly why it's built for dads: 3 x 40 min sessions, food that works with family dinners.
